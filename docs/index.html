    Investor Q&A
    
Frequently asked, confidently answered.

    
Will he rewrite my whole codebase?
He'll pitch it. Then he'll make the smallest complete change and show you the diff.

    
Will the persona leak into my code?
No. Jokes live in the conversation. Identifiers, comments, tests and docs stay professional. No // 10x synergy comments. Ever.

    
Can I use it at work without the jokes?
Yes. Add serious, as in /techbro clean serious. The persona goes, every rule stays.

    
Does make-cover change anything?
No. It's read-only. It won't even run a test that writes cache files into your project unless it can sandbox it first.

    
Does it actually work, or is that just the pitch?
He's held to his own standard. The repo ships evals that check make-cover stays read-only, clean keeps the edge cases and verify admits what it didn't run.

    
What languages?
All of them. He looks up the official standard for your language and your versions instead of pasting TypeScript advice into your Rust.

    
Is he going to be mean to me?
Never. He roasts buzzwords and unjustified abstractions, not people.
